# Beelzebub icon concept sketches

Five 256×256 PNG concepts generated as conceptual placeholders. Each is themed around the Beelzebub namesake (Lord of the Flies / demon prince of gluttony), the V Rising vampire aesthetic, and the mod's ability-acquisition mechanic.

**These are programmatic concept sketches, not final artwork.** They're meant to help pick a *direction* before commissioning a real illustrator (or generating something with a proper art tool). Any of them is good enough to ship a first Thunderstore version, but a hand-illustrated version will read far better.

| # | File | Concept | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01_chalice_of_devouring.png` | Gold chalice filled with blood, flies orbiting | Direct take on "Lord of the Flies + gluttony"; chalice is the vampire-feeding motif |
| 2 | `02_vampire_skull.png` | Bone skull with glowing red eyes and prominent fangs | Most readable at thumbnail size; lowest creative risk |
| 3 | `03_demonic_sigil.png` | Inverted pentagram in a red sigil ring | Pure occult/magic motif; reads as "skill acquisition" via ritual |
| 4 | `04_winged_initial.png` | Stylized gold "B" with black bat wings | Brandable mark; strongest "Beelzebub" identity without literal demon imagery |
| 5 | `05_devouring_vortex.png` | Spiral of red orbs being pulled into a central void | Literal visual metaphor for the mod's gameplay (abilities being devoured) |

## How to use

Pick one (or commission something better), drop it as `Beelzebub/Beelzebub/icon.png` (256×256 PNG), and the next `tcli build` will pick it up.
