🩸 Now Recruiting: Testers for Beelzebub 🩸

Hey Testers!  Our new server side mod will hopefully breath new life in a well loved game while we wait for the new VRising game to be developed. The premise: defeat a unit— any V-Blood or NPC — and steal its abilities. Build a collection across the whole bestiary and turn your enemies abilities into your personal arsenal.

What you'll play with:
- ⚔️ Steal abilities from enemies — V-Bloods and NPCs. 
- 💀 Summon minions that fight for you
- 🗡️ Per-weapon loadouts, bindable hotkeys, tunable spells

What we ask of testers: report bugs clearly (what you did / what happened / what you expected), and share feedback on balance and fun (ex: Ability OP, Ability non-functional, etc.)

⚠️ Read first — this is essentially an Alpha/Beta version on a dev server. Bugs and crashes are expected (that's the point), and your progress — or the whole server — may be wiped at any time without notice for updates or to address issues. Don't get attached; come to experiment, not to settle in.

Thank you for your time and assistance! 
Where to start-Please review "Testers Start Here" and use tags on every post please.
